## Editing DNA to reuse

1. **Allow coherent gameplay to breathe.** Do not reflexively optimize for high cuts-per-minute.
2. **Maintain one visual running gag** (e.g. death/failure counter), tied to real events.
3. **Reuse a short original interruption motif** at moments of comic reset or failure. A strong motif is recognizable on later appearances.
4. **Use occasional oversized visual punchlines** only when the gameplay justifies them.
5. **Keep the outro a separate, replaceable module** with original branding.
